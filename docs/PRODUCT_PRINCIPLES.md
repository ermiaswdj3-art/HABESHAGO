# HABESHAGO Product Principles

## Mission

HABESHAGO exists to help people and goods move across Ethiopia through one intelligent, trusted, and user-friendly mobility ecosystem.

## Product Promise

HABESHAGO should make every important mobility task feel simple, fast, and reliable.

Users should focus on where they want to go or what they want to deliver. HABESHAGO should help them choose the most suitable way to accomplish that goal.

---

## 1. Goal First

Users should begin with their destination or intended task.

Instead of forcing users to choose between separate services first, HABESHAGO should understand their goal and recommend suitable options.
